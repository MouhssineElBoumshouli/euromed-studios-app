# 0003 · Visual direction "Studio Light"

- **Status:** approved by Mouhssine (13 Nov 2026)
- **Replaces:** the typography and dark-navy look in 0002 (the brand colors in 0002 still apply)

## What we decided

The app follows the look of the earlier Figma made with ChatGPT and Muse, which Mouhssine prefers, and pushes it further:

- Light, airy background with soft lilac shapes.
- Big, bold, friendly titles in sentence case (Plus Jakarta Sans). Body text in DM Sans. Both are free Google fonts, bundled in the app.
- Deep Euromed navy (`#1C2B6B`) for main buttons and selected states. Bright blue for highlighted words. Gold only as a small touch on navy.
- White rounded cards with soft shadows. Studio photos used large.
- A floating pill tab bar. Round outlined back buttons.
- Motion with a purpose: slides between booking steps, a sliding tab highlight, live dots, cards that settle in. All motion is turned off when the phone asks for reduced motion.

## Why

Mouhssine found the first build (navy, condensed uppercase titles) not nice. He asked us to copy the old Figma's direction and make it much better.

## What did not change

Business rules, data and screen flows. Only the look and layout.
