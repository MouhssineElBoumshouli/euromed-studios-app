# Euromed Studios App

The official mobile app for **Euromed Studios**, the audiovisual center of Euromed University of Fes (UEMF).

People use it to book studios and equipment, get their bookings approved, open the door with a QR code, follow video projects from script to final file, and report broken equipment.

UEMF asked for this app. Our contact is **Brahim Seddouki, Director of Communication**.
